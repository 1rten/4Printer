# Hardware Specifications & Layout Reference

## 1. Frame Specifications (Workstation V19.2)

- **Overall Dimensions**: 1400 mm (W) × 800 mm (D) × 700 mm (H)
- **Extrusion Standard**: 4040 Black Anodized Aluminium Profile (40 × 40 mm cross-section)
- **Compartments**:
  - **Left Bay**: 700 mm width (accommodates drawer system and laser tray)
  - **Right Bay**: 580 mm width (dedicated to Bambu Lab P1S)
  - **Mid-Height Reinforcement**: Z = 360 mm
  - **Drawer Support Beams**: Z = 520 mm

## 2. Fasteners & Connectors

- **Anti-Vibration Brackets (🔴 Red)**: 20 pcs heavy-duty corner brackets (35×28×35 mm / 28×35×35 mm).
- **Countersunk Through-Hole Anchor (🔵 Cyan)**: 12 pcs vertical-to-horizontal central bore tie-ins (Z=40, Z=660).
- **T-Slot Anchor Pins (🟣 Purple)**: Surface slot anchoring along X and Y axes.
- **Heavy Slides (⚪ Silver)**: 600 mm stroke heavy-duty rails installed outboard at X=160 mm and X=590 mm.
